About ScamShield

A student project on Cyber Security — built to protect people from mobile scams.

How it works

1

Input

You paste a message, link, or QR code.

2

Analyze

ScamShield scans it instantly — on your device, nothing is sent anywhere.

3

Verdict

You get a plain-language verdict: Safe, Suspicious, or Dangerous.

4

Educate

Tap 'Learn Why' on any warning to understand exactly what the scammer was trying.

🔒

Your privacy

Everything runs on your computer. We never send your messages to any server, never open any link, and never store more than the first 60 characters of any scan.

Technical Architecture

🔍 Detection Pipeline

Combines deterministic regex threat matrices across 10 categories with Levenshtein-based brand lookalike detection and homoglyph resolution.

📱 QR & UPI Verification

Parses UPI string protocols, extracts beneficiary VPA and payment attributes, and warns against reverse-charge collection fraud.

🌐 Bilingual Devanagari PDF

Native text shaping using HarfBuzz and bundled Noto Sans Devanagari font enables flawless Hindi export without character clipping.
